W1 Human Robots

The Jam Sandwich Robot

Practical Life Alignment: Sequence of Work & Grace and Courtesy

Big Idea & Core Principle

Computers strictly execute literal instructions without human intuition or context.

Setup & Environment Prep

Set up a clean demonstration table at the front of the circle rug.

Key Materials Required

Primary Focus (Ages 3–6)

1-step physical actions; physically perform step before giving command to Teacher Robot.

Elementary Focus (Ages 6–12)

Pair work writing precise written algorithm on paper prior to live testing on Teacher Robot.

Steps to Facilitate Activity

Control of Error (Self-Correction)

The teacher acts as a literal robot—executing instructions exactly as spoken or written.
