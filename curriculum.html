CS Unplugged Scope & Sequence (24-Week Dual-Track Matrix)

Organized into three 8-week phases with dedicated, parallel activities for both Primary (3–6) and Elementary (6–12) classrooms every week.

Phase 1 • Fall

Algorithms & Procedures

Parallels Great Lesson 1 (Laws of Universe). Focuses on sequencing, literal instructions, and debugging.

Phase 2 • Winter

Data Representation

Parallels Great Lessons 2 & 3 (Life & Human Tools). Base-2 binary, pixel graphics, and data compression.

Phase 3 • Spring

Information Theory & Security

Parallels Great Lessons 4 & 5 (Writing & Numerals). Sorting networks, parity error checking, and ciphers.
